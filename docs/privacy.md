# Privacy notice (implementation draft)

Docsup stores account identity, family membership, document metadata, document versions, audit events, and optional notifications to provide the vault. Documents are private to the family space and are not used for advertising or model training by Docsup.

Optional AI features may transmit temporary document content to a configured third-party provider. The provider, model, retention controls, and purpose must be shown before processing and must be configured for an appropriate privacy/no-training policy. AI output can be wrong and is never official verification.

Users can delete documents through trash and configured retention. Legally required audit records may persist as non-content security records. Operators should replace this draft with jurisdiction-specific legal review, a data processing inventory, retention schedules, and user-rights workflows before launch.
